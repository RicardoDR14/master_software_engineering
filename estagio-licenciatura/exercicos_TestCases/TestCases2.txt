Scenario: E-commerce Shopping Cart

You are testing the shopping cart feature of an e-commerce website. The shopping cart has the following functionality:
1. Users can add multiple items to the cart.
2. Each item has a quantity and price.
3. The cart automatically updates the total price whenever items are added, removed, or updated.
4. Users can update the quantity of items or remove items from the cart.
5. Users can apply discount codes that reduce the total price.
6. There’s an option to proceed to checkout, where the cart is validated before being submitted.

Discount Codes:
- DISCOUNT10: 10% off the total price.
- DISCOUNT20: 20% off the total price (cannot be used in combination with DISCOUNT10).
- FREESHIP: Provides free shipping (removes the shipping cost).

Shipping Costs:
- Standard shipping costs $5.
- If the user applies a discount code of DISCOUNT10 or DISCOUNT20, the shipping cost should still be applied (unless FREESHIP is also applied).

Task:
Create test cases that cover all of the following aspects:

1. Basic Functionality:
   * Adding items to the cart.
   * Updating quantities of items in the cart.
   * Removing items from the cart.
2. Price Calculation:
   * Verifying that the total price is updated when items are added, removed, or updated.
   * Verifying that the correct price is calculated when discount codes are applied.
   * Verifying that the shipping cost is applied correctly.
3. Discount Code Application:
   * Applying valid discount codes and ensuring the price updates correctly.
   * Verifying that discount codes can’t be used together (e.g., DISCOUNT10 and DISCOUNT20).
   * Verifying that invalid or expired discount codes result in an error message.
4. Checkout Process:
   * Ensuring that users cannot proceed to checkout if the cart is empty.
   * Verifying that the cart validation works and shows the correct error messages when there’s an issue with the cart (e.g., no shipping address, invalid items).
   * Verifying the proper redirection after successful checkout.
